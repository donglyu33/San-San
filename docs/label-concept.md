# San San label direction

Status: design concept for owner review; not a print-ready or legally reviewed label.

Warm cream-white paper, black typography, ample whitespace and one deep-red 三 seal. Pair a spaced serif SAN SAN wordmark with a large sauce name and the confirmed slogan “One Spoon, Endless Ways.” Keep this hierarchy consistent across sauces. A black lid and transparent glass complete the direction.

public/label-concept.svg is original vector artwork, intentionally illustrated. The artwork and website caption identify it as a concept, not actual product photography. It makes no promises about jar size, contents or finished packaging. The existing picture-led homepage is preserved. The label concept appears in a smaller section after the main imagery and catalog, with the confirmed slogan also displayed as live text above the hero. The legacy hero artwork still contains its earlier tagline; revising its embedded text is separate artwork work.

Before production: owner approval of the visual direction, actual jar and closure selection, supplier label dimensions, physical print proof, and verified back-label information with appropriate professional review. No ingredients, allergens, nutrition, net weight, storage directions or certifications have been invented.

Existing catalog images still need authenticity review. Real product photography should be used only after final products and packaging are approved.
